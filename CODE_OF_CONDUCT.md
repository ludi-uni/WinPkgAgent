# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of respectful behavior include using welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, public or private abuse, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may clarify, warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participation. The maintainers will protect the reporter's confidentiality where practical.
